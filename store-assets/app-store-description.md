## Subtitle

## Description

Transform your life with iTrackHabit - the beautifully designed habit tracker that works offline and helps you build lasting positive routines.

**Key Features:**

🎯 **Smart Habit Tracking**
- Track daily, weekly, or custom frequency habits
- Set specific targets and track progress
- Beautiful neumorphic design that's easy on the eyes

📱 **Offline-First Design**
- Works perfectly without internet connection
- Your data stays private and secure on your device
- Optional cloud sync when you're online

📊 **Detailed Analytics**
- Track your progress with beautiful charts
- View completion rates and streaks
- Get personalized insights about your habits

🏆 **Motivation & Achievements**
- Earn badges for consistency
- Build streaks and celebrate milestones
- Share progress with friends (optional)

✨ **Premium Experience**
- Clean, modern interface with smooth animations
- Dark mode support
- No ads, no distractions

Whether you want to drink more water, exercise regularly, meditate daily, or build any positive habit, iTrackHabit makes it simple and enjoyable.

Start building better habits today!
